03 / MAKE SPACE

The art of recovery.

A little quiet movement goes a long way.

✳
01 / AFTER TRAINING12 MIN

Reset & restore

Unwind your hips, shoulders, and back after a session.

  1. Easy breathing 1 min
  2. Cat–cow 2 min
  3. Low lunge, both sides 3 min
  4. Thread the needle, both sides 2 min
  5. Child's pose 2 min
  6. Rest on your back 2 min
02 / ON A REST DAY25 MIN

Move & breathe

A slower full body flow to make room for the next week.

  1. Breathing and gentle neck rolls 3 min
  2. Cat–cow and child's pose 4 min
  3. Low lunge and half split, both sides 6 min
  4. Standing forward fold and mountain pose 4 min
  5. Seated twist, both sides 4 min
  6. Rest on your back 4 min

04 / EVERYDAY FUEL

Eat to feel good.

No rigid rules. Just useful habits you can return to.

GOOD FOOD
GOOD WORK
GOOD DAYS
01

Build a balanced plate

Make room for vegetables or fruit, a source of protein, and satisfying whole grains or other fiber-rich foods.

02

Drink throughout the day

Keep water nearby and drink more when you are active or it is hot. Let thirst and your routine guide you.

03

Plan the easy option

Keep simple staples on hand: eggs or beans, frozen vegetables, fruit, yogurt, and a grain you enjoy.

04

Make room for enjoyment

Consistency comes from meals that fit your life, culture, budget, and appetite.
